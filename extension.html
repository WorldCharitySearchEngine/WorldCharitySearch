Or set it up yourself in under a minute

Chrome, Edge, Brave
  1. Open Settings → Search engine → Manage search engines.
  2. Next to Site search, click Add.
  3. Name: World Charity Search, Shortcut: wcs, URL:
    https://worldcharitysearchengine.github.io/WorldCharitySearch/search.html?q=%s
  4. Click the ⋮ menu next to it and choose Make default.
Firefox
  1. While on this page, click the search icon at the left of the address bar and choose Add “World Charity Search”.
  2. Open Settings → Search and pick World Charity Search as your default.
Safari

Safari only allows a fixed list of search engines. Bookmark our homepage and set it as your homepage instead.
