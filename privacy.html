Privacy Policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

World Charity Search is built to collect as little as possible. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use our website and browser extension.

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account. We do not set cookies, and we do not store your searches, your IP address or any profile of you.

Your searches

When you search, your search terms are sent straight to our search partner, which shows you the results. Your browser connects to our partner directly, so the partner receives your search terms, IP address and the browser information every website receives. The partner, and the advertisers it works with, handle that information under their own privacy policies. Our current search partner is Google; see Google's Privacy Policy.

The browser extension

The extension sets World Charity Search as your default search engine and shows our published donation totals. It does not read the pages you visit, does not collect your browsing history, and does not send us any data. It requests no special browser permissions.

Services this site uses

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.
  • Google Fonts provides the typeface, so your browser requests it from Google.
  • Every.org pages open when you click a charity; Every.org's own privacy policy applies there.

Children

World Charity Search is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, for example when we add a new search partner or privacy-friendly visitor counts, we will update this page and the date at the top.
